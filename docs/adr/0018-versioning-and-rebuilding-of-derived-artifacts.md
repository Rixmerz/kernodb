## Context

Non-transactional indexes, embeddings, summaries, groupings, inferred relations, caches and learned optimizations are **derived artifacts**: they are computed from source data and can become obsolete. ADR-0007 covers the freshness of indexes and ADR-0011 the metadata of embeddings; a common contract for all derivatives is missing, one that says how they are versioned, when they become obsolete and how they are rebuilt.

## Options considered

1. **Contract per derivative type**, each with its own rules. Flexible, but it repeats provenance and suppression logic in every place.
2. **Common derivatives registry** (proposal): every derivative carries a descriptor with sources (exact unit and revision, or a sequence range), method or model and version, parameters that affect the result, date, input hash, state (`ready`, `building`, `stale`, `failed`, `superseded`) and whether it is rebuildable.
3. **Derivatives immutable, recalculated only in full**. Simple, but unworkable for costly embeddings and summaries.

## Decision criteria

1. Each derivative can be linked to its sources and marked obsolete when they change (I-38, I-25).
2. A rebuildable derivative produces the same result as a new one over the same data, except for non-deterministic methods declared as such (I-24).
3. Suppressing a source invalidates or suppresses its derivatives according to ADR-0009.
4. A summary never replaces the original when auditing is required (spec §10).
5. Incompatible models and dimensions are not mixed (spec §11).

## Evidence

None yet. M0 and Phase 1 do not generate derivatives of this kind.

## Decision

**Accepted on 2026-11-12 by the owner: option 2 (common derivatives registry), under the terms of this section.** Derivatives generated by external models are produced behind adapters or jobs outside the transactional core (I-31); the core only stores the result and its descriptor. A change of model or version creates new derivatives (`superseded` for the earlier ones) instead of rewriting them. The logical export distinguishes source data from rebuildable derivatives (spec §14).

## Consequences

- The descriptor is part of the logical format and is versioned (ADR-0003, ADR-0008).
- Detecting obsolescence requires that writing a source be cheap to cross with its derivatives: an inverse index source → derivatives is needed.
- Non-deterministic methods (for example, some embedding models) must declare the hash of the output, not only that of the input, for verification.

## Conditions for reopening

A derivative type whose cost of descriptor or of inverse index is disproportionate to the derivative itself.

## Follow-up

- Apply the contract to the Stage 4 indexes and the Stage 6 optimizations before the Stage 8 embeddings.
- End-to-end test: modify and suppress a source and check the state of each derivative.
