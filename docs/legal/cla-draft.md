# Contributor licence agreement (CLA) proposal

> **DRAFT PROPOSAL. NOT IN FORCE.**
>
> - It is not legal advice and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It needs that review.
> - It also needs the approval of the project owner. Without it, it is not used.
> - Until it is approved, **no external contribution is accepted** (ADR-0019; decision D15 of the [Stage 9 record](../architecture/stage9-record.md)).
> - It is not linked from the README as if it were in force, and no `CONTRIBUTING.md` refers to it.
> - Direction (b) confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-19; the text is still pending legal review and is not in force. See [docs/owner-decisions.md](../owner-decisions.md).

## 1. Scope

This agreement covers contributions that a person sends to Kerno (the "Project") for inclusion: code, documentation or other material (the "Contribution"). "Owner" means the holder of the Project: [name pending, to be declared by the owner].

## 2. Copyright licence

1. You keep the authorship and the ownership of your Contribution. This agreement does not transfer authorship.
2. You grant the Owner a **non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free** licence to use, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute and publicly communicate the Contribution, by any means now known or later devised.
3. The licence includes the right to **sublicense** the Contribution and to **relicense it under other terms**, including commercial terms that differ from the Project's public licence.
4. The Project's public licence (MIT OR Apache-2.0, as stated in its README and `Cargo.toml`) does not change under this agreement. This agreement adds to the Owner the licence in section 2.
5. It does not oblige you to license your Contribution exclusively to anyone, and it does not stop you from using it in other projects.

## 3. Patent licence

You grant the Owner and the recipients of the Project a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free patent licence under the patents you hold or can license that are necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone, or by its combination with the Project as you delivered it. The licence ends if you start litigation alleging that the Project infringes a patent of yours because of the Contribution.

[Scope of this section, pending review by the lawyer.]

## 4. Statements of the contributor

You state that:

a) you have the right to make the Contribution, and if you produced what you contribute as part of work for an employer that has rights over it, you have its permission;

b) the Contribution is your own work, or you clearly state in the submission the origin and the licence of any third-party part, and that licence is compatible with the Project's licence;

c) you know of no claim affecting the Contribution, except any you point out in the submission.

## 5. Rights you keep

You keep your rights in the Contribution. You may use it, publish it or license it to third parties, subject only to the non-exclusive licences in sections 2 and 3.

## 6. No warranty

The Contribution is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including warranties of title, non-infringement, merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

## 7. How agreement is given

You accept this agreement, in the version in force when you submit, in one of these ways:

a) a `Signed-off-by: <name> <email>` line at the end of each commit of the Contribution; or

b) a statement in the description of the pull request that cites the version of this agreement and says that you accept it.

The Owner keeps a record of these acceptances. There are no external contributions accepted today, and this draft does not regularise any.

## 8. Version and entry into force

- This draft is not in force. It will enter into force when the Owner approves it in writing, with a version number and a date. It applies only to Contributions sent after that date.
- A later change to the text is a new version. A contribution is governed by the version cited when it was sent.
