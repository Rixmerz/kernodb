# Invariants and risks

Status: in force at the closure of Phase 1 (2026-10-29); the engine's invariants are verified on `kerno-storage`. §2.1 (IU-1…IU-10) was promoted and approved by the owner on 2026-11-04, at the closure of Stage 3; I-21 (relations), I-23, I-24, I-32 (applied to indexes) and I-39 (declared relations) are promoted in the closure review E4-05 (2026-11-05, [stage4-closure.md](stage4-closure.md)), **approved by the owner on 2026-11-05**; I-32 (planner part) and I-37 are promoted in the closure review E5-05 (2026-11-06, [stage5-closure.md](stage5-closure.md)), **pending the owner's approval**; I-33…I-36 (result cache, Stage 6) are promoted in the closure review E6-05 (2026-11-11, [stage6-closure.md](stage6-closure.md)), **pending the owner's approval**; I-29 is promoted for `kerno-storage` at the closure of the Stage 9 preview (E9A-06, 2026-11-12, [stage9-early-closure.md](stage9-early-closure.md)), **approved by delegation of the owner on 2026-11-12, revocable** · Related to: [SAD §3, §17, §18](SAD.md), [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-transaction-model-and-durability.md), [failure matrix](failure-matrix.md)

This document lists the properties that **no later feature may break** and how they are verified today. An invariant without an automated test is marked as *pending*: it is not enough for it to appear in the documentation (SAD §18.2).

The identifiers are stable: ADRs, tests and PRs must cite them (`I-03`, `R-S2`).

## 1. Invariants of the storage engine

The tests cited live in two places. The **shared suites** of the harness ([`crates/kerno-testkit/src/harness/`](../../crates/kerno-testkit/src/harness)) are generated by `engine_test_suite!`, and since Phase 1 they run over `kerno-storage` in [`crates/kerno-storage/tests/shared.rs`](../../crates/kerno-storage/tests/shared.rs) (modules `shared_functional`, `shared_crash`, `shared_faults` and `shared_corruption`, for `KernoDb` and for the profiles `inline`, `m0_profile` and `serial`); the same battery keeps running over the M0 spikes and over `LogEngine`. The engine's own tests (`tests/*.rs` of `kerno-storage`) are cited by name. Only what each engine declares about itself changes: `buffered_power_loss_prefix` (whether a consistent prefix is required in `Buffered` on power loss; `kerno` declares it `true`, the M0 B+ tree does not) and `detects_truncation`. The columns "M0" and "Phase 1" indicate the state at the closure of the spike and at the closure of Phase 1.

### 1.1 New invariants of Phase 1

I-16 to I-19 were proposed by [phase1-design.md §8](phase1-design.md) and WP14 promotes them to the table above, with the `kerno-storage` tests that verify them. The existing I-01, I-03, I-04, I-09, I-11, I-12 and I-15 are extended in their own row. The state of each one is backed by the [Phase 1 report](../testing/f1-report.md) (sweeps of 1000 seeds, real `kill -9` and the failure matrix); what that report leaves **pending** (the LazyFS record and the freezing of the format) does not change the state of any invariant, but it does change that of risks R-S2 and R-S3 of §3.

## 3. Risks

The product risks are in [SAD §19](SAD.md#19-risks-and-mitigations). The risks specific to the spike and to the verification method are added here.

| ID | Risk | Consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-S1 | The prototypes are not production engines; their figures also reflect the quality of a few days' implementation. | Choosing for an implementation detail and not for the architecture. | Compare each prototype with a mature engine of the same family (redb for CoW B+ tree, fjall for LSM) and reason about the difference; see ADR-0012. |
| R-S2 | The failure model of `SimVfs` does not match exactly a real file system (ext4 `data=ordered`, `rename` semantics, `fsync` errors, controller cache). | Excessive trust in the recovery. | Deliberately pessimistic model (reordering and torn writes at 512 B or at byte level). In Phase 1, `SimVfs` adds as options the Linux semantics after a failed `fsync` (`FsyncFailure::LinuxDropPages`), per-directory durability (`DirDurability::PerDir`), disk full (`set_space_limit`, `fill_disk_after`) and process death that leaves unsynchronised data pending (`CrashKind::ProcessKillKeepPending`), walked by `extended_failure_suite!`; real `kill -9` of a writer with `StdVfs` is covered by `process_kill.rs` (200 iterations per mode in the Phase 1 output). **The record of a test with a device that loses pages is still pending** (`spikes/storage/run-lazyfs.sh`, `.github/workflows/lazyfs.yml`): the `SimVfs` model has not yet been checked against a file system that discards unsynchronised pages. |
| R-S3 | The `fdatasync` latency of the spike's VM (p50 ~0.1 ms, p99 ~0.6 ms) does not represent consumer SSDs, network disks or laptops. | Conclusions about small commits that do not carry over. | The report publishes the measured `fdatasync` latency; the conclusions are expressed in `fsync` per commit and amplification, not only in commits/s. The Phase 1 output measurement was taken on the same class of VM as M0 because the owner has not yet named the target machine (C-09): **still open** to repeat it on the target hardware ([F1 report](../testing/f1-report.md)). |
| R-S4 | Latency peaks from compaction (LSM) or from tree growth (B+ tree) that a median hides. | Unforeseen tail latencies in Cauce. | Report p95/p99/p999/max and repeat runs. |
| R-S5 | CRC32C detects accidental corruption, not deliberate tampering. | False sense of integrity against an attacker with disk access. | Cryptographic integrity (AEAD) is decided in ADR-0006; the threat model makes it explicit. |
| R-S6 | The spike's contract uses batches without reading inside the transaction. | Underestimating the cost of read-modify-write transactions. | ADR-0002 defines the transactional API; `kerno-storage` implements `begin_write` (WP13b) and `rmw_counters` measures its cost (T9, [F1 report](../testing/f1-report.md)). |
| R-S7 | Licences: copying design or code from reference projects. | Legal risk. | The prototypes are own implementations; the reference engines are only used as dependencies in the benchmark. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`. |
